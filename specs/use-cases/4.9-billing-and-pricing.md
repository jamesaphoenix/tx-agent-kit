# 4.9 Billing and Pricing

> **Parent:** [requirements.md](../requirements.md)
> **Detailed spec:** See [`../design/billing-and-pricing-design.md`](../design/billing-and-pricing-design.md)
> for data model, webhook invariants, credit ledger semantics, local-dev bootstrap, and UI status.

---

tx-agent-kit's revenue model has two clear customer-facing parts:

1. A **flat-rate monthly subscription** for platform access, storage allocation, rate-limit
   headroom, support tier, and unlimited members.
2. A **prepaid credit wallet** for AI usage and storage overage. Customers explicitly top up
   credits, enable auto-recharge, or receive a one-time welcome/local-dev credit. Credits never
   expire.

The subscription does **not** bundle recurring AI credits. AI, video, image, voice, and storage
overage usage is charged from the wallet at provider cost plus a **10% infrastructure markup**.
The credit ledger is the source of truth for balances and history.

## Monthly Charge Structure

```
Customer cost model:
├── Plan subscription (fixed)                  ← platform access + storage allocation
└── Prepaid credits consumed as usage happens  ← AI/video/image/voice/storage overage
```

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Included storage | Welcome credit | Members |
|------|-------|------------------|----------------|---------|
| Try Me | $19/mo | 10 GB | $9 one-time | Unlimited |
| Pro | $49/mo | 100 GB | $20 one-time | Unlimited |
| Agency | $199/mo | 500 GB | $45 one-time | Unlimited |

Annual billing gives 2 months free. The welcome credit is granted once per organization lifetime
after a successful first subscription charge. The local-dev bootstrap can activate a Pro-like
state and grant a single $20 local welcome credit without Stripe checkout, but only outside
staging/production.

## Usage-Based Pricing (AI & Video)

LLM costs and video generation costs are passed through to the customer with a **10% markup**.
Costs are represented as `CostResult` records in decimillicents and can be aggregated while
preserving per-operation attribution.

OpenRouter-routed generation uses OpenRouter's reported cost directly. Custom calculations are
only needed for non-OpenRouter providers such as fal.ai, Veo/Veo3 video, and ElevenLabs audio.

Usage-based charges apply to:
- AI content generation (concept creation, caption writing, asset tagging)
- Video rendering (Veo3, Remotion)
- Image generation
- Voice generation
- Automated campaign execution
- Any AI operation triggered from the human UI

All balance mutations are recorded in the append-only credit ledger for transparency and
auditability. Credits are measured in **decimillicents** for precision.

## Storage Billing: Prepaid Credits + Hard Ceiling

Each plan includes a storage allocation. Uploads that exceed the included storage consume prepaid
credits at the storage overage rate. No postpaid storage debt is created.

At the hard storage ceiling, uploads are rejected with HTTP 402 even if the org has credits. This
prevents runaway storage cost. Existing assets remain readable.

| Limit | Try Me | Pro | Agency |
|-------|--------|-----|--------|
| Included storage per org | 10 GB | 100 GB | 500 GB |
| Storage overage rate | $0.10/GB from credits | $0.10/GB from credits | $0.08/GB from credits |
| Hard storage ceiling | 20 GB | 200 GB | 1 TB |
| Max single file size | 50 MB | 50 MB | 50 MB |

### What the User Sees

1. At **80% usage** → dashboard warning + email to org admin.
2. At **100% included storage** → overage is deducted from credits before upload completion.
3. At **zero available credits** → upload is rejected with a top-up/upgrade message.
4. At **hard ceiling** → upload is rejected regardless of credit balance.

### Configurable Retention Policies

Org admins can adjust retention periods via **Settings → Storage → Retention Policies**. Before
confirming changes, the system shows a forecast of projected storage usage, estimated overage
costs, and a recommendation to upgrade if the new policy would exceed the plan cap.

| Asset type | Default | Options |
|------------|---------|---------|
| Published renders | 90 days after published to all intended platforms | 90 days / 6 months / Permanent |
| Failed/cancelled renders | 90 days | 90 days / 6 months / Permanent |
| Soft-deleted assets | 90 days | 90 days / 6 months / Permanent |
| Source uploads (images, audio) | Permanent | 90 days / 6 months / Permanent |

After render deletion, the platform post reference (post ID, URL) is retained in the dashboard.

## Usage Caps

The organization monthly spend cap is an optional user-defined guardrail. If no cap is set, usage
is governed by available credits and subscription state. Campaign-level caps are still planned but
remain deferred until the campaigns subsystem requires them.

### Organization Monthly Usage Cap

When the org approaches or hits the configured cap:

| Threshold | What the User Sees |
|-----------|-------------------|
| 80% of cap | Dashboard warning + email to org admin |
| 95% of cap | Urgent warning + email |
| 100% of cap | All AI operations blocked. All campaigns paused. Email + in-app notification. |

**Resume options:** Admin raises/removes the cap, waits for the monthly reset, or tops up credits
if the operation was also credit-blocked.

## Billing UI

Admins with `manage_billing` can use dedicated billing routes:

- `/org/{orgId}/billing` — overview, current plan, balance, quick actions, local-dev activation.
- `/org/{orgId}/billing/plans` — plan comparison and Stripe checkout confirmation.
- `/org/{orgId}/billing/history` — paginated append-only credit ledger.
- `/org/{orgId}/billing/usage` — usage summaries, category breakdowns, spend cap progress, and
  layout-stable skeletons during loading.
- `/org/{orgId}/billing/settings` — billing email, payment method portal, auto-recharge,
  spend cap, welcome credit history.

The desktop sidebar also shows the current credit balance for the active organization.

## Decisions (Confirmed)

- No free tier.
- No free trial.
- No seats; members are unlimited on all plans.
- No bundled recurring AI credits.
- Credits never expire.
- Usage markup is 10%.
- Org spend cap is optional and user-set.
- Campaign budgets are deferred until campaigns need them.
- Storage billing is prepaid from credits with hard storage ceilings.
- Storage retention: configurable per-org with cost forecast before changes.
- Stripe webhooks must be idempotent, stale-event-gated, and BigInt-safe for cents conversion.
- All money-moving Stripe SDK calls must carry idempotency keys.
