# 4.10 Team and Role Management

> **Parent:** [requirements.md](../requirements.md)

---

Team admins can invite users, assign roles, and configure granular permissions. The permission
system covers actions such as: creating/editing/deleting media, scheduling posts, viewing/creating/
editing/deleting campaigns, and managing billing.

Teams also store brand settings (colours, typography, tone of voice) that influence AI-generated
content.

## Member Invitations

Admins invite people by email. The invitee does not need to already have an account. If the email
belongs to an existing user, the invitation is linked to that user immediately. Otherwise it stays
pending by normalized email and becomes visible after a user signs up with the same email.

The invite form supports:

- **Access level:** `member` or `admin`.
- **Member type:** internal teammate or client collaborator.
- **Workspace access:** all workspaces or a specific workspace/team.

Client collaborators are account-backed members for ongoing workspace-scoped collaboration.
They are separate from stateless content-review links, which still require no account.

## Safety Guards

- The organization owner cannot remove themselves without transferring ownership.
- The last active admin cannot be demoted, disabled, or removed.
- Bulk removal skips the signed-in user and surfaces a specific notification instead of silently
  succeeding with no effect.
- Member-management dialogs submit on Enter when valid and close on Escape through the shared
  Dialog primitive.
